# Tatsu privacy policy

Effective date: 2026-10-06

Tatsu does not collect, transmit or share any data. The app has no network access, no account system, no analytics and no tracking.

## What the app stores

Tatsu keeps a list of up to 100 kanji you recently opened or copied. It is stored on your device (SwiftData on iPhone, iPad and Mac; Jetpack DataStore on Android), is never synced or uploaded, and is visible only to the app. On Android the recent list never leaves the device and is not included in Google backups (the app sets `allowBackup="false"`).

To delete it, right-click (Mac) or long-press (iPhone, iPad and Android) an item and choose Remove from history, or use Clear in the Recent list. Deleting the app removes all of its data; on Android you can also clear it in Settings › Apps › Tatsu › Storage › Clear data.

## Network and third parties

The app makes no network requests. On Android the app does not even request the Internet permission. The kanji dictionary is bundled inside the app. Tatsu contains no third-party SDKs, advertising, analytics or tracking code, and no third party receives data from it.

## Children

Tatsu collects no data from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this URL with an updated effective date. The full history is in the repository's git log.
