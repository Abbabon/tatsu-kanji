# Tatsu privacy policy

Effective date: 2026-09-29

Tatsu does not collect, transmit or share any data. The app has no network access, no account system, no analytics and no tracking.

## What the app stores

Tatsu keeps a list of up to 100 kanji you recently opened or copied. It is stored on your device with SwiftData, is never synced or uploaded, and is visible only to the app.

To delete it, right-click (Mac) or long-press (iPhone and iPad) an item and choose Remove from History, or use Clear in the Recent list. Deleting the app removes all of its data.

## Network and third parties

The app makes no network requests. The kanji dictionary is bundled inside the app. Tatsu contains no third-party SDKs, advertising, analytics or tracking code, and no third party receives data from it.

## Children

Tatsu collects no data from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this URL with an updated effective date. The full history is in the repository's git log.
